# Experiment template

## Hypothesis
State one falsifiable claim.

## Methodology
Describe baselines, test cases, controls, and statistical treatment.

## Environment
Record revision, toolchain, OS, CPU/GPU, driver, configuration, and relevant flags.

## Metrics
Define units, collection method, and acceptance criteria before running.

## Results
Provide raw data links, summary, and observed variance.

## Limitations
List confounders, precision limits, and unsupported cases.

## Reproduction
Give exact commands, fixtures, seeds, and expected output format.

## Conclusion
Say whether the evidence supports the hypothesis and what remains unknown.
